Our Services

DreamDrive SA provides different car rental services for customers with different transportation needs.

Our services are designed for personal travel, business trips, holidays and longer rental periods.

Short-Term Car Rentals

Our short-term rental service is suitable for customers who need a vehicle for a few days or a short period.

This service is suitable for weekend trips, temporary transportation and short business trips.

Long-Term Car Rentals

Our long-term rental service is designed for customers who need a vehicle for an extended period.

Customers can enquire about the available vehicles and rental period.

Business Rentals

Business rentals provide transportation for professionals and small businesses.

Vehicles can be used for business meetings, work-related travel and temporary business transportation.

Holiday Rentals

Holiday rentals are suitable for customers travelling for holidays and family trips.

Customers can choose a vehicle based on the number of passengers and the amount of luggage required for their trip.

Rental Requirements

Customers will need to provide the required information when making a rental enquiry.

  • Full name
  • Contact number
  • Email address
  • Preferred vehicle
  • Rental start date
  • Rental end date
